Research

Vision-Based Drone Sense-and-Avoid System

Ongoing research and coursework project building a real-time computer vision perception pipeline for UAVs and urban air mobility (UAM) platforms. The system is designed to detect aerial obstacles under edge-device constraints and enable autonomous collision avoidance using deep learning — addressing one of the core technical challenges facing safe, scalable drone and air taxi operations.

Urban Air Mobility: Technical and AI Challenges

An independent research paper examining the technical and AI-driven challenges facing urban air mobility — covering autonomous navigation, sense-and-avoid systems, airspace management, and the broader engineering obstacles to deploying aerial autonomous vehicles safely at scale.
